Principal Investigator

Chen Ling, PhD

Associate Professor & Doctoral Supervisor

School of Life Sciences, Xiamen University

Dr. Chen Ling completed postdoctoral research at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL) from 2019 to 2023. The lab focuses on understanding "how" and "why" in microbial metabolism through synthetic biology approaches, leveraging metabolic engineering, multi-omics analysis, and genome editing tools to engineer microbial systems for sustainable production of chemicals and materials.
