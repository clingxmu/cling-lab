🎓 Postdoctoral Positions

We are always looking for talented postdoctoral researchers in synthetic biology, metabolic engineering, and genome editing. Interested candidates should have a PhD in Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Chemical Engineering, or related fields. Please send your CV and research interests to chenling@xmu.edu.cn.

🎒 PhD and Master's Students

We welcome PhD and master's students interested in our research areas. Students should have a background in biology, chemistry, or engineering. Please include your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of research interests when applying.

🤝 Collaborations

We are open to research collaborations with academic and industry partners. Please contact Dr. Chen Ling to discuss potential collaboration opportunities.
