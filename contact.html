Join Our Lab

🎓 Postdoctoral Positions

We are always looking for talented postdoctoral researchers in synthetic biology, metabolic engineering, and genome editing. Interested candidates should have a PhD in Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Chemical Engineering, Computer Science, or related fields. Please send your CV and research interests to cling@xmu.edu.cn.

🎒 PhD and Master's Students

We welcome PhD and master's students interested in our research areas. Students should have a background in biology, chemistry, or computer science (计算机). Please include your CV, transcripts, and a brief statement of research interests when applying.

🤝 Collaborations

We are open to research collaborations with academic and industry partners. Our published work on genome editing tools have been patented. Please contact Dr. Chen Ling to discuss potential collaboration opportunities.
